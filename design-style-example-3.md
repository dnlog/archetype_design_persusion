## Design notes

- **Lover:** Warm sensory language, a shared unhurried coffee ritual, and intimate human expressions make the offer feel affectionate and pleasurable without turning it into status or seduction.
- **Modern:** The two-column functional grid, broad window panes, horizontal structure, simple geometric forms, natural materials, daylight, and restrained ornament draw on the documented Mid-Century and International Style characteristics. The architecture and clear product information carry the look rather than an added visual filter.
- **Liking:** The brand speaks like a friendly host (“stay for one more”) and reflects the audience’s relaxed coffee ritual in a candid, relatable scene. The invitation is warm, the product details are direct, and no testimonial or personal relationship is fabricated.
- **Palette:** Cream, wood, muted leaf green, and a restrained rose accent give the scene tactile warmth while keeping the layout calm and legible.
