## Design notes

- **Everyman:** Plainspoken copy, an affordable everyday staple, familiar daily settings, and inclusive sizing make the shirt feel useful and welcoming rather than exclusive or aspirational.
- **Modernism:** The hero uses a functional two-column grid, clear hierarchy, geometric framing, horizontal bands, restrained ornament, and a limited palette. The graphic is built from simple forms and clean lines; its structure does the visual work.
- **Unity:** Four different people share the frame on equal footing and wear the same uncomplicated shirt. “A place in the picture” and “made for the everyday, together” make belonging the promise; the CTA invites the visitor into the group without pressure.
- **Palette:** Ink blue anchors legibility, warm off-white echoes the product, muted green grounds the scene, and brick red and ochre act as small, purposeful accents.
